Privacy Policy

Daily Grace · Published by Tae Kim · Updated November 12, 2026

Daily Grace is a personal app for reading one verse from the World English Bible (WEB) each day. It does not collect, transmit, store on a server, or sell personal information. There is no account and no analytics.

Information that stays on your device

If you use the app, it can save these things on your phone or tablet:

  • Verses you mark as saved
  • Whether the daily reminder is on, and the time you chose
  • Whether you have already seen the reminder prompt
  • Your appearance choice: system, light, or dark

This information is stored only on the device, with the app’s local storage. It is not uploaded to Tae Kim, who publishes Daily Grace. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Notifications

If you allow reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device for the days ahead. The verse text in the reminder is chosen on your phone. Daily Grace does not operate a push-notification server and does not receive a device token.

On Android, the app can restore those scheduled reminders after the phone restarts. That also happens on the device.

You can turn reminders off in the app at any time. You can also turn off notifications for Daily Grace in your phone’s system settings.

Sharing

When you share a verse, the app opens your device’s share sheet, either with the verse as text or as an image. Whatever you send goes to the app you pick, such as Messages or Mail. Daily Grace does not see or store that activity.

If you tap Instagram Story or Facebook Story, the app opens that app with a picture of the verse, when it is installed. If it is not installed, the phone’s share sheet opens instead. Daily Grace does not receive your social account, your password, or what you post.

Photos

If you tap Save to Photos, the app asks permission to add a picture. It adds only the wallpaper you just made. It does not browse your camera roll, and it does not upload the picture.

On iPhone, sharing a story to Instagram can also ask for photo library access, because that is how the picture is handed to Instagram. Daily Grace still does not look through your photos.

Data we do not collect

  • No name, email address, or phone number
  • No account or login
  • No location
  • No contacts
  • No photos, other than a verse image you choose to save or share yourself
  • No advertising identifier
  • No analytics, and no crash reports sent to us
  • No third-party trackers

The app works offline. The verses are included in the app.

Children

Daily Grace does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will replace this page and the date above will change. The same text is included inside the app under Settings, then Privacy policy.
